The number of measles deaths among children under five started very high in 2000 with about 756000 deaths globally. It increased over the next two years and hit a maximum in 2002 with about 848000 deaths, which is the highest number in the whole record.

After 2002, the number started decreasing strongly. It dropped to about 603000 in 2004, 462000 in 2006 and about 279000 in 2009. The decline continued until the number reached about 148000 deaths in 2012.

After 2012, the number stopped decreasing as steadily and kept moving up and down. It reached about 190000 in 2013, dropped close to 105000 in 2016 and rose again to about 169000 in 2019. At 2020, it witnessed another dip and reached about 104000 deaths, which is the lowest number in the record. It then increased to about 151000 in 2021.

Overall, under-five measles deaths decreased hugely from the early 2000s until around 2012, then kept hovering at much lower levels with several rises and dips.
