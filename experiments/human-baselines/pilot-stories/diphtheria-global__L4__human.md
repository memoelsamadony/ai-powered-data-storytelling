The diphtheria cases reached about 30000 globally in 2025, which is the highest number recorded in the whole 26-year period. The previous two years were also very high, with about 25000 cases in both 2023 and 2024. This shows that the most recent years had a much higher number of cases compared with most of the earlier period.

The incidence rate followed a similar trend but did not reach its maximum in 2025. It reached 4 cases per million in 2025, while the highest incidence rate was recorded in 2023 with 4.2 cases per million.

Looking back at the earlier years, the situation was very different. Between 2006 and 2013, the incidence rate stayed below 1 case per million, while the number of diphtheria cases was also much lower. In 2015, for example, there were only about 4500 reported cases, compared with around 30000 in 2025.

Overall, the diphtheria cases were relatively low during the middle part of the record but increased significantly during the later years. The biggest numbers appeared near the end of the dataset, especially between 2023 and 2025.
