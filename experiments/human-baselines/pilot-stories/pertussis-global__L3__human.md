The pertussis cases started at about 190000 in 2000 and kept moving up and down over the next two decades. During the period from 2000 to 2019, the number never went below about 117000 and never went above about 250000 cases.

This changed in 2020 when the number dropped sharply to about 70000 cases. It decreased even further in 2021 and reached only about 30000 cases globally, which is the lowest number in the whole 26-year record. The cases then started rising again, reaching about 64000 in 2022 and 163000 in 2023.

The biggest change happened in 2024. The number witnessed a massive spike and reached about 942000 cases with an incidence rate of 137.1 per million, making it the highest year by a huge amount. In 2025, the number decreased sharply to about 265000 cases.

Overall, the pertussis cases were quite steady for many years, then dropped to a minimum in 2021 before rising very quickly and hitting a huge maximum in 2024.
