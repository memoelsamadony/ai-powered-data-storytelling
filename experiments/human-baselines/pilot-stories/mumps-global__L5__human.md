The mumps cases were quite high during most of the period from 2000 to 2018. The number started at about 544000 cases in 2000 and reached its highest point in 2011 with about 727000 cases. During these years, the cases moved up and down but were usually above 300000.

At 2019, the number witnessed a huge dip and reached only about 170000 cases, which is the lowest number in the record. It then rose to about 279000 in 2020, decreased a bit in 2021, and started increasing again until it reached about 388000 cases in 2023.

The next two years witnessed another decrease. Cases dropped to about 245000 in 2024 and about 235000 in 2025. The incidence rate in 2025 was 41.2 per million, which is the lowest rate in the whole dataset.

Overall, mumps cases were generally much higher in the earlier years and became lower after 2019, even though there were some rises in between.
