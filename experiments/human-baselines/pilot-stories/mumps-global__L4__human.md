The mumps incidence rate started extremely high in 2000 at about 317 cases per million. The number of reported cases that year was about 544000. During the following years, both the cases and the rate kept changing, with the cases eventually hitting a maximum of about 727000 in 2011.

The number then started decreasing and reached about 312000 in 2014. It rose again for a few years before witnessing a huge dip at 2019, when only about 170000 cases were reported. After that, cases increased again to about 388000 in 2023.

The last two years show another decrease. The number dropped to about 245000 cases in 2024 and then about 235000 in 2025. The incidence rate also fell from 95.2 per million in 2023 to 67.2 in 2024 and finally 41.2 in 2025.

Overall, the mumps data shows many ups and downs, but the incidence rate became much lower over time and reached its minimum in 2025.
