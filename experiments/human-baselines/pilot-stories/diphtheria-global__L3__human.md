The world recorded about 11600 diphtheria cases in 2000. This number kept decreasing over the next few years until it reached only 4333 cases in 2006, which is the lowest number in the whole 26-year record. The cases then stayed mostly below 6000 every year until 2013, while the incidence rate remained below 1 case per million during this period.

After 2015, the number started increasing almost every year. It went from about 4500 cases in 2015 to 7100 in 2016 and about 8800 in 2017. Then, the number witnessed a huge spike in 2018, reaching about 17000 cases, and increased even further in 2019 to about 23000.

The next three years were much lower, with the number staying around or below 10000 cases. However, another huge rise happened in 2023 when the number reached about 25000 cases with an incidence rate of 4.2 per million, which is the highest incidence rate in the record.

The number continued increasing slightly in 2024 before reaching about 30000 cases in 2025, the highest number of diphtheria cases across all 26 years. Overall, the dataset shows a long period of relatively low cases followed by a strong increase after 2015.
