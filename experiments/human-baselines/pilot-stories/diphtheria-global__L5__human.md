The diphtheria cases reached about 30000 globally in 2025, which is the highest number recorded since 2000. This was a noticeable increase from 2024, when the number was about 25000 cases.

The three highest numbers in the dataset happened during the last three years. The number reached about 25000 in 2023, stayed almost the same in 2024, and then increased further to about 30000 cases in 2025.

However, the number did not increase continuously throughout the whole period. After reaching about 23000 cases in 2019, it witnessed a huge dip in 2020 and dropped to about 10000 cases. The number stayed around or below 10000 until 2022 before it more than doubled in 2023.

The lowest number in the whole record happened in 2006, with only 4333 cases and an incidence rate of 0.7 per million. In comparison, the incidence rate reached 4 per million in 2025, while the maximum incidence rate was 4.2 per million in 2023.

Overall, diphtheria cases went through several increases and decreases over the years, but the biggest rise happened during the later part of the dataset, ending with the maximum number in 2025.
