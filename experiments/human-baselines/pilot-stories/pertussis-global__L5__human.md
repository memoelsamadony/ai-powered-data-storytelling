The world recorded about 265000 pertussis cases in 2025. This was a huge decrease compared with 2024, when the number reached about 942000 cases, which is the highest number in the whole record.

Before this spike, pertussis cases were much lower. From 2000 until 2019, the number mostly kept hovering between about 117000 and 250000 cases. The cases then dropped sharply to about 70000 in 2020 and reached their lowest point in 2021 with only about 30000 cases.

After 2021, the number started increasing again. It reached about 64000 in 2022 and about 163000 in 2023 before suddenly surging to the massive 2024 peak. The incidence rate followed the same pattern, going from only 4.6 per million in 2021 to 137.1 per million in 2024.

Overall, pertussis cases had a relatively stable early period, followed by a huge dip, a very strong rise to the 2024 maximum, and then a sharp decrease again in 2025.
