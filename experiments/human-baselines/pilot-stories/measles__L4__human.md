The measles cases were at their highest listed level in 1980 with about 3.9 million cases globally and only 16% first-dose vaccination coverage. The number of cases then kept decreasing over the years while vaccination coverage increased, reaching 73% in 1990 and 84% in 2010.

By 2015, the number had dropped to about 215000 cases, which was much lower than the early years. However, the cases started rising again and hit about 359000 in 2018 before witnessing a huge spike to about 873000 in 2019.

The number then dropped sharply and reached about 123000 in 2021. After that, the trend changed again. Cases increased to around 207000 in 2022 and then jumped to about 669000 in 2023. They stayed nearly the same in 2024 at about 676000 cases. Vaccination coverage in 2024 was 84%, compared with its listed maximum of 86% in 2018 and 2019.

Overall, the long-term trend shows a huge decrease in measles cases, but the recent years show that cases have started increasing strongly again since 2021.
