The world recorded about 544000 mumps cases in 2000 with an incidence rate of about 317 cases per million. During the next decade, the number kept rising and falling, but it stayed quite high and reached a maximum of about 727000 cases in 2011.

After that, the number started decreasing more clearly. It dropped to about 312000 cases in 2014, went back above 500000 between 2016 and 2018, and then witnessed a huge dip in 2019 to only about 170000 cases, which is the lowest number in the dataset.

The low number did not remain for long. Cases increased again and reached about 388000 in 2023. The next two years then witnessed another decrease, reaching about 245000 in 2024 and 235000 in 2025.

Overall, mumps cases were much higher during the first half of the record and became lower in the later years. The incidence rate also followed a downward trend and reached its lowest level in 2025 at 41.2 per million.
