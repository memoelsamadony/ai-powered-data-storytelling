The measles cases started very high in 1980 with about 3.9 million cases globally. Over the next decades, this number decreased significantly, reaching about 1.3 million in 1990, around 585000 in 2005 and about 344000 in 2010. The first-dose vaccination coverage increased during the same period from 16% in 1980 to 84% in 2010.

The cases reached about 215000 in 2015 before increasing again. At 2019, the number witnessed a huge spike and reached about 873000 cases. It then decreased sharply over the next two years, hitting about 159000 in 2020 and only 123000 in 2021.

After 2021, the cases started rising again. They reached about 207000 in 2022 and then jumped to about 669000 in 2023. The number remained almost the same in 2024 at about 676000 cases, while vaccination coverage stood at 84%.

Overall, measles cases decreased greatly compared with the early years of the dataset, but the recent years show a noticeable rise again after the low point in 2021.
