The diphtheria cases were pretty low during the period between 2006 and 2013. During these years, the number never went much above 5600 cases, and the lowest number was recorded in 2006 with only 4333 cases globally. The incidence rate was also low during most of this period, staying around 0.7 to 0.9 cases per million.

However, this low number did not continue. The cases started increasing and reached about 17000 in 2018. They increased even further in 2019, hitting about 23000 cases. After this spike, the number decreased sharply to about 10000 cases in 2020 and then dropped again to about 8600 in 2021. It slightly increased in 2022 to around 10000 cases.

The next three years witnessed a huge increase. The number jumped to about 25000 cases in 2023, remained almost the same in 2024, and then increased again to about 30000 cases in 2025, which is the highest number in the whole record.

Overall, diphtheria cases stayed low for many years, especially between 2006 and 2013, but started rising strongly during the later years. By 2025, the incidence rate reached 4 cases per million.
