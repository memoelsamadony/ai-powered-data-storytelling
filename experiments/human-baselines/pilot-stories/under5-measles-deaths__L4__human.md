The under-five measles deaths started at about 756000 in 2000 and increased for the next two years, reaching a maximum of about 848000 deaths in 2002. After that, the number started a long and strong decrease.

The deaths dropped to about 543000 in 2005, 345000 in 2007 and around 255000 in 2011. By 2012, they had fallen further to about 148000. This shows that most of the major decrease happened during the first 12 years of the record.

After 2012, the number started hovering at a much lower level and kept going up and down. It reached about 190000 in 2013, about 105000 in 2016 and then increased for the next three years until it reached about 169000 in 2019.

The number dropped again in 2020 to about 104000 deaths, which is the lowest value in the dataset, before rising back to about 151000 in 2021.

Overall, under-five measles deaths decreased massively from the early 2000s, but the later years show more fluctuations and no clear continuous decline.
