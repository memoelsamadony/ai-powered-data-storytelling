The measles deaths among children under five were very high at the beginning of the record. The number was about 756000 in 2000 and then increased to about 848000 in 2002, which is the highest number across all the listed years.

After 2002, the number kept decreasing significantly. It reached about 603000 in 2004, 462000 in 2006 and about 279000 in 2009. By 2012, it had dropped to about 148000 deaths, meaning the number became much lower than it was at the beginning.

After 2012, the decrease was no longer steady. The number kept hovering between roughly 100000 and 190000 deaths. It rose to about 169000 in 2019, then witnessed a huge dip in 2020 and reached about 104000, which is the lowest number in the whole record. In 2021, it increased again to about 151000 deaths.

Overall, the dataset shows a huge long-term decrease in under-five measles deaths, especially before 2012, followed by a period of smaller rises and dips at a much lower level.
