The highest number of measles deaths among children under five happened in 2002, when the number reached about 848000 deaths globally. After this maximum, the deaths started decreasing strongly over the following years.

By 2007, the number had dropped to about 345000 deaths and then went below 300000 in 2009. The decline continued until it reached about 148000 in 2012, which was a huge difference compared with the early 2000s.

After 2012, the trend became less clear. The number increased to about 190000 in 2013, dropped to about 110000 in 2014, and kept going up and down over the next years. It reached around 105000 in 2016 and then increased gradually to about 169000 in 2019.

At 2020, the number witnessed a dip and reached about 104000 deaths, the lowest in the whole record. It then rose again in 2021 to about 151000.

Overall, most of the huge decrease happened before 2012. After that, the number stayed much lower but kept fluctuating instead of continuously decreasing.
