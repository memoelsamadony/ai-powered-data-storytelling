The pertussis cases stayed within a fairly similar range during the period between 2000 and 2019. Most years had between about 117000 and 250000 cases globally, with some spikes such as about 245000 in 2004 and 250000 in 2012.

The pattern changed strongly in 2020. Cases dropped to about 70000 and then witnessed another huge dip in 2021, reaching only about 30000 cases, which is the lowest number in the whole record. The number increased a little in 2022 and then went back to about 163000 in 2023.

At 2024, the cases witnessed an extremely huge spike and reached about 942000 cases. This was much higher than every previous year, and the incidence rate also hit 137.1 per million. The next year, the number decreased sharply to about 265000 cases with an incidence rate of 37.3 per million.

Overall, pertussis cases were quite stable for around two decades, dropped heavily around 2020-2021, then suddenly surged to a record high in 2024 before falling again in 2025.
