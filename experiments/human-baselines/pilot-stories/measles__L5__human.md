The world recorded about 676000 measles cases in 2024, which is the highest number since 2019. Just a few years earlier, the number had reached about 123000 cases in 2021, which is the lowest number among the listed years.

Most of this recent increase happened after 2022. The cases went from about 207000 in 2022 to about 669000 in 2023, causing the number to more than triple in one year. At 2024, it stayed almost the same at about 676000 cases.

The longer record looks very different. In 1980, there were about 3.9 million reported measles cases globally and first-dose vaccination coverage was only 16%. Over time, coverage increased strongly and reached 84% in 2024, while the number of cases became much lower than the early years.

Overall, measles cases witnessed a huge long-term decrease, but the number started rising again after 2021 and stayed quite high in 2023 and 2024.
