The mumps cases started very high in 2000 with about 544000 cases globally and an incidence rate of about 317 per million. The number kept moving up and down during the following years, reaching more than 650000 cases in 2004 and then hitting its highest number in 2011 with about 727000 cases.

After 2011, the cases generally started decreasing. They fell to about 312000 in 2014, increased again for a few years, and then witnessed a huge dip in 2019 when they reached only about 170000 cases, which is the lowest number in the whole record.

The cases rose again after 2019 and reached about 388000 in 2023. After that, they decreased for the next two years, hitting about 245000 in 2024 and about 235000 in 2025. The incidence rate in 2025 reached 41.2 per million, which is the lowest rate in the whole 26-year record.

Overall, mumps cases changed a lot over the years, but the general trend moved downward after the high levels seen in the earlier part of the dataset.
