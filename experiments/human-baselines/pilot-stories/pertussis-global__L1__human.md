The pertussis cases started at about 190000 globally in 2000 and then decreased for the next few years until they reached about 117000 in 2003. At 2004, the number witnessed a noticeable spike and reached about 245000 cases before decreasing again.

From 2005 until 2019, the cases mostly kept hovering between about 125000 and 250000. The number then witnessed a huge dip in 2020, falling to about 70000 cases, and decreased even further in 2021 to only about 30000, which is the lowest number in the whole record.

After that, the cases started increasing again. They reached about 64000 in 2022 and about 163000 in 2023. At 2024, the number surged massively and reached about 942000 cases, which is by far the highest number in the dataset. The incidence rate also jumped to 137.1 per million.

In 2025, the cases dropped sharply to about 265000, but this was still higher than all the years before 2024.

Overall, pertussis cases stayed relatively stable for many years, dipped strongly in 2020 and 2021, then witnessed a huge spike in 2024 before falling again in 2025.
