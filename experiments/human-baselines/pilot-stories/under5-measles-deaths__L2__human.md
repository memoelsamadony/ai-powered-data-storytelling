The under-five measles deaths were extremely high at the beginning of the dataset. They started at about 756000 deaths in 2000 and increased to about 803000 in 2001 before reaching a maximum of about 848000 deaths in 2002.

The number then started decreasing almost continuously. It fell to about 603000 in 2004, 462000 in 2006, 345000 in 2007 and about 263000 in 2010. By 2012, the number had reached around 148000 deaths, showing a huge decrease compared with the early years.

From 2013 onward, the number kept moving up and down instead of continuing the same steady decline. It stayed between about 104000 and 190000 deaths. The lowest number happened in 2020 with about 104000 deaths, but it increased again to about 151000 in 2021.

Overall, measles deaths among children under five witnessed a huge decrease during the first half of the record, but after 2012 the number became more stable and kept fluctuating at a much lower level.
