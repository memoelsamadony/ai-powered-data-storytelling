The measles cases were extremely high at the beginning of the record, reaching about 3.9 million cases globally in 1980 while the first-dose vaccination coverage was only 16%. The number then kept decreasing over the next years, reaching about 1.3 million in 1990 and about 720000 in 1995. Vaccination coverage increased strongly during the same period and reached 73% by 1990.

The cases continued decreasing in the following years and reached about 215000 in 2015. They then started increasing again, hitting about 359000 in 2018 and witnessing a huge spike in 2019 with about 873000 cases. After that, the number dropped sharply to about 159000 in 2020 and reached its lowest point in the listed years at about 123000 in 2021.

The cases started rising again after 2021. They increased to about 207000 in 2022 and then surged significantly to about 669000 in 2023. In 2024, the number remained almost the same at about 676000 cases, while vaccination coverage was 84%.

Overall, measles cases decreased hugely from 1980 until the 2010s, but the number witnessed strong rises again in 2019 and between 2022 and 2024.
