The diphtheria cases started at about 11600 cases globally in 2000. The number generally decreased over the next few years until it witnessed a huge dip in 2006, reaching only 4333 cases, which is the lowest number in the whole 26-year record.

After that, the number of cases stayed pretty low for several years and remained below 8000 until 2015. However, the cases started increasing again after 2015. They reached about 17000 cases in 2018 and increased even further in 2019, hitting about 23000 cases.

The number then witnessed a sharp decrease in 2020 and 2021, reaching about 10000 and 8600 cases. It stayed close to 10000 in 2022 before it suddenly increased again in 2023 to about 25000 cases. The number stayed around the same level in 2024 before hitting a maximum of about 30000 cases in 2025.

Overall, diphtheria cases decreased greatly during the first years of the record and stayed relatively low for several years. After 2015, the number started increasing strongly, with some decreases in between, until it reached its highest number in 2025. The incidence rate in 2025 was 4 per million, while the highest incidence rate was 4.2 per million in 2023.
