The mumps cases kept hovering at high numbers during the first years of the dataset. They started at about 544000 cases in 2000 and reached a maximum in 2011 with about 727000 cases globally.

After this peak, the number generally decreased. It reached about 516000 in 2013 and dropped further to about 312000 in 2014. The cases increased again between 2015 and 2018, but then they witnessed a huge dip in 2019 and reached only about 170000 cases, which is the lowest number in the whole record.

The cases rose back over the next few years and reached about 388000 in 2023. However, the number then decreased again to about 245000 in 2024 and around 235000 in 2025.

The incidence rate showed an even clearer long-term decrease. It was about 317 per million in 2000 and reached only 41.2 per million in 2025, which is the lowest rate in the dataset.

Overall, mumps cases did not decrease in a straight line, but the later years are generally much lower than the earlier years.
