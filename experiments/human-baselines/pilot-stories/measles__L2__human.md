The measles cases reached their lowest number in the recent period at 2021 with about 123000 cases globally. At the same time, first-dose vaccination coverage was 81%. This low number did not last for long, as the cases increased to about 207000 in 2022.

At 2023, the number witnessed a huge spike and reached about 669000 cases, which is more than three times the number from the previous year. It remained almost the same at 2024 with about 676000 cases. Vaccination coverage also increased a bit to 84%, but it was still below the roughly 95% level usually associated with herd immunity.

Looking further back, measles cases used to be much higher. In 1980 there were about 3.9 million cases while vaccination coverage was only 16%. The cases generally decreased as coverage increased over the following decades.

Overall, the measles cases showed a huge long-term decrease, but the recent period changed direction. After reaching a low in 2021, the number rose sharply again and stayed high in 2023 and 2024.
