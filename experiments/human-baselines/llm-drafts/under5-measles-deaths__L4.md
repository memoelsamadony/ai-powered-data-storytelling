# Global under-5 measles deaths fell by 70 percent, then stopped falling

In 2000, 756,332 children under five died of measles worldwide. The count climbed to 848,272 in 2002, the highest in this 22-year series, and then fell hard: by 2011 it was 254,599, a drop of 70 percent from the peak.

Then the decline stalled. Every year from 2012 through 2021 has landed between 103,901 and 190,190 deaths, and the count rose in six of those nine year-to-year steps. The 104,776 recorded in 2016 was followed by three straight increases, reaching 168,887 in 2019.

The two most recent years repeat the pattern. 2020 recorded 103,901 deaths, the lowest figure anywhere in the table. 2021 came in at 151,463, up 46 percent in a single year and the highest total since 2019.

That 2021 total is still under a fifth of the 2002 peak. The table lists counts only, with no rate per million, so it shows how many children died, not how common those deaths were.
