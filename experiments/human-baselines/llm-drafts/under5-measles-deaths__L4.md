# Measles deaths in under-fives fell 70 percent by 2011, then went sideways

Measles killed 756,332 children under five in 2000, and the toll kept climbing for two more years, to 848,272 in 2002. Nothing in the 22 years on this table is higher. What followed was a long slide: 254,599 by 2011, 70 percent below the peak.

After 2011 the count doesn't so much fall as wander. Every year from 2012 through 2021 lands between 103,901 and 190,190, and six of the nine year-to-year steps went up rather than down. Three straight rises followed 2016's 104,776, ending at 168,887 in 2019.

The two most recent years wobble the same way. 2020 came in at 103,901, the lowest anywhere in the table; a year later, 151,463, up 46 percent and the biggest total since 2019.

Set against 2002, the 2021 figure is under a fifth of the peak. And the table counts deaths only, with no per-million column, so it shows how many children died, not how common that was.
