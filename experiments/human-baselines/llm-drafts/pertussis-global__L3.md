# Global pertussis cases hit a 26-year high in 2024, then fell sharply

For two decades the numbers held to a narrow range. Between 2000 and 2019, reported pertussis cases moved between 116,598 and 250,330 and never broke out. Then came 2021, the lowest point in the whole span: 30,402 cases, 4.6 per million.

The climb back was fast. 64,313 cases in 2022, 163,400 in 2023, then 941,893 in 2024. That last figure is nearly six times the 2023 total and close to four times the previous high of 250,330, set in 2012. Incidence hit 137.1 per million, the highest in the series.

Then, just as abruptly, the line came down. 2025 recorded 265,317 cases, roughly 72 percent below the 2024 peak, at 37.3 per million.

That is still the second-highest total in the twenty-six years on record, close to nine times the 2021 low and higher than every year from 2000 through 2023.
