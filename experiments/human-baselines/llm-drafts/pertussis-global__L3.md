# Global pertussis swung from a 26-year low to a 26-year high in three years

For two decades the global pertussis count stayed inside one band. Between 2000 and 2019 it never fell below 116,598 and never climbed above 250,330. The band broke in 2020, at 69,605 cases, and 2021 went lower still: 30,402, or 4.6 per million, the smallest figure anywhere in these twenty-six years.

What came back was not the old range. 64,313 cases in 2022. 163,400 in 2023. In 2024, 941,893, nearly six times the 2023 count and close to four times the 250,330 that had stood as the series high since 2012. Incidence reached 137.1 per million.

Coming down took one year. 265,317 cases in 2025, 37.3 per million, about 72 percent under the peak.

Small only beside 2024, though. Nothing else in the table comes near 265,317. It runs nearly nine times the 2021 low and sits above every total from 2000 through 2023.
