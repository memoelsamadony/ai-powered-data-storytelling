# Diphtheria's 26-year record high landed in 2025

For most of the 2000s and 2010s, reported diphtheria cases worldwide stayed quiet. Nothing between 2006 and 2013 topped 5,700 in a single year, and 2006 sits at the bottom of the whole 26-year record with 4,333 cases. Five of those eight years registered just 0.7 cases per million people.

The quiet didn't hold. By 2018 the annual total had reached 16,911, and 2019 pushed it to 22,989. It sank again after that: 10,137 in 2020, 8,659 in 2021, 10,027 in 2022.

Now look at where the three biggest years in the series sit. They are the three most recent: 24,782 in 2023, 25,147 in 2024, and 30,205 in 2025. The 2025 count runs roughly a fifth above 2024 and comes close to seven times the 2006 low. Against population, incidence has moved from 0.7 per million to 4.
