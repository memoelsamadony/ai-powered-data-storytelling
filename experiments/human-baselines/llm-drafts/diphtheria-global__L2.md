# Diphtheria cases are the highest in 26 years of global records

For most of the 2000s and 2010s, reported diphtheria cases stayed low. Between 2006 and 2013 the global total never topped 5,700 cases in a year, and 2006 marked the low point of the whole record at 4,333. Incidence sat at 0.7 per million in five of those years.

Then the line turned. Cases reached 16,911 in 2018 and 22,989 in 2019, before dropping back to 10,137 in 2020 and 8,659 in 2021.

The three highest years in the series are the three most recent ones. From 10,027 in 2022, the count jumped to 24,782 in 2023, then 25,147 in 2024, then 30,205 in 2025. That last figure is about 20 percent above 2024 and nearly seven times the 2006 low. Measured against population, incidence has gone from 0.7 per million to 4.
