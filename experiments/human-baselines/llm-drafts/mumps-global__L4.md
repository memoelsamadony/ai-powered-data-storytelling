# Global mumps rate falls to its lowest level in 26 years of data

The world reported 234,954 mumps cases in 2025, at a rate of 41.2 per million people. That rate is the lowest in a series that runs back to 2000, edging below the previous low of 43.4 per million set in 2021.

The long arc is steep. In 2000 the rate stood at 316.8 per million, the highest year in the series. The 2025 figure is 87 percent lower. The decline was not smooth: the rate climbed back to 196 per million in 2011, the same year the case count peaked at 726,638.

Recent years have zigzagged. The rate rose from 43.4 per million in 2021 to 95.2 in 2023, then fell in each of the two years since, down 57 percent to 2025.

Counts and rates do not always move together. In 2020 the world logged 279,270 cases, 64 percent more than in 2019, yet the rate that year was lower, 66.4 against 81.7.
