# Global mumps rate falls to 41.2 per million, the lowest in the record

The global mumps rate has never been lower in this record than it was in 2025, at 41.2 per million on 234,954 reported cases. The series runs 26 years, back to 2000. Only 2021 has been close, at 43.4.

The high end of the record is 316.8 per million, in 2000. Getting from there to 41.2 is a drop of 87 percent, though nobody reading the years in between would call it steady. By 2011 the rate had worked back up to 196 per million, and that same year carries the table's largest case count, 726,638.

The recent stretch isn't tidy either. From 43.4 in 2021 the rate more than doubled, reaching 95.2 by 2023, then shed 57 percent over the two years since.

Counts alone read differently. In 2020 the world logged 279,270 cases, 64 percent above 2019, yet the rate that year came in below the one before it, 66.4 against 81.7.
