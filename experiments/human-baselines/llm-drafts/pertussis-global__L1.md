# Global pertussis cases hit 941,893 in 2024, nearly six times the 2023 count

Reported pertussis cases worldwide hit 941,893 in 2024. Nothing in the series, which runs back to 2000, comes near it. Cases numbered 163,400 in 2023, so the total multiplied 5.8 times in a single year, and it ran nearly 31 times the series low of 30,402 in 2021.

Incidence for the year was 137.1 cases per million. No earlier year in the table tops 41.1 per million, recorded in 2004.

Cases then dropped. The 2025 count of 265,317 came in 72 percent under 2024, and incidence eased to 37.3 per million. That still leaves 2025 second only to 2024, a little above the 250,330 logged in 2012.

Before 2020 the series held a narrow band, every annual total between 116,598 and 250,330. Totals fell to 69,605 in 2020 and 30,402 in 2021, then rose in each of the next three years.
