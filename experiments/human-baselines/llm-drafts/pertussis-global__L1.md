# Global pertussis cases hit 941,893 in 2024, the highest since at least 2000

Reported pertussis cases worldwide reached 941,893 in 2024, the highest total in a series running back to 2000. The count was 5.8 times the 163,400 cases reported in 2023, and nearly 31 times the series low of 30,402 in 2021.

Incidence reached 137.1 cases per million in 2024. The previous peak was 41.1 per million in 2004.

Cases fell sharply the following year. The 2025 total of 265,317 was 72 percent below 2024, and incidence dropped to 37.3 per million. Even so, 2025 remains the second-highest year in the table, above the 250,330 cases recorded in 2012.

Before 2020, every annual total fell between 116,598 and 250,330. Totals then dropped to 69,605 in 2020 and 30,402 in 2021, and climbed in each of the next three years.
