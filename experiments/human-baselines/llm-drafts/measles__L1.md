# Measles cases hit 675,533 in 2024, most since 2019

Measles reports worldwide came to 675,533 in 2024. Nothing since 2019 has been higher. The gain over 2023 is slight, 6,450 cases on a base of 669,083. Go back to 2021 and the distance widens: that year logged 123,152, the lowest in the series, and 2024 runs more than five times that year's total.

Longer records cut the other way. Reported cases in 1980 came to 3,852,242, putting 2024 some 82 percent below. First-dose coverage moved from 16 percent then to 84 percent in 2024. Its high was 86 percent, in 2018 and 2019. Herd immunity takes about 95 percent, eleven points above the 2024 figure.

India reported 18,530 cases in 2024, Nigeria 14,999, Germany 645. Adjusted for population, Nigeria comes out ahead. Its 65.8 per million ran five times India's 12.9 and nearly nine times Germany's 7.6. Coverage stood at 97 percent in India, 96 in Germany, 57 in Nigeria.
