# Global measles cases hit 675,533 in 2024, highest since 2019

Reported measles cases worldwide reached 675,533 in 2024, the highest total since 2019. That is up slightly from 669,083 in 2023 and more than five times the 123,152 reported in 2021, the lowest year in the series.

The 2024 total is 82 percent below the 3,852,242 cases reported in 1980. First-dose vaccination coverage rose from 16 percent in 1980 to 84 percent in 2024. It peaked at 86 percent in 2018 and 2019. Herd immunity requires about 95 percent first-dose coverage, eleven points above the 2024 level.

Country figures for 2024 show India with 18,530 cases and Nigeria with 14,999. Measured per million people, Nigeria's rate of 65.8 was five times India's 12.9 and nearly nine times Germany's 7.6. Germany reported 645 cases with 96 percent coverage, India 97 percent and Nigeria 57 percent.
