# Pertussis fell by nearly three quarters in 2025 and still ranked fourth-highest since 2000

2024 was the series high: 137.1 cases per million, 941,893 reported worldwide. Nothing since 2000 comes near it. Set that against the 2000-2019 average of roughly 27 per million and the peak is about five times over.

Then 2025 came in at 37.3 per million, 265,317 cases, down about 73 percent in a single year. Steep. And still the fourth-highest reading in the table: only 2024, 2004 at 41.1 and 2012 at 39.6 sit above it. Every year from 2013 through 2023 was lower.

The floor sits at 2021: 4.6 per million, 30,402 cases. Rates had been sliding before that, 23 per million in 2019, 9.9 in 2020. Afterwards they came back, 9 in 2022, 22.7 in 2023, and then the 137.1. Trough to peak, that's close to thirty times.

Counts and rates are all this table holds. What moved them is not in it.
