# Global pertussis rates fell by nearly three quarters in 2025, after 2024's series high

In 2024, reported pertussis reached 137.1 cases per million people worldwide, 941,893 cases in all. That is the highest rate in a series running back to 2000, and about five times the 2000-2019 average of roughly 27 per million.

2025 brought a steep drop: 37.3 per million, 265,317 cases, down about 73 percent from the previous year. Even so, only three years in the table sit higher: 2024, 2012 at 39.6 and 2004 at 41.1. Every year from 2013 to 2023 was lower.

The series' low point came in 2021 at 4.6 per million, 30,402 cases. Rates had already slid from 23 per million in 2019 to 9.9 in 2020, then climbed back through 9 in 2022 and 22.7 in 2023 to the 2024 peak, nearly thirty times the 2021 rate.

The table records case counts and rates only, not what drove them.
