# Pertussis fell hard in 2025 and still landed second highest since 2000

For two decades the global pertussis count barely left its lane. Every year from 2000 to 2019 landed somewhere between 116,598 and 250,330 cases. Three quiet years interrupted that: 69,605 in 2020, then 30,402 in 2021, the lowest anywhere in this table, then 64,313 in 2022.

2023 climbed back to 163,400, inside the old range. What arrived in 2024 was not a return to normal. 941,893 reported cases. Read that again. Close to four times the previous record, 2012's 250,330, and around 31 times the 2021 low. Incidence reached 137.1 per million, more than triple any other year on the list.

So 2025 reads as a big step down: 265,317 cases, a fall of about 72 percent in twelve months. Smaller than 2024, still enormous. Across the 26 years here only 2024 ran higher, and 2025 clears the old 2012 peak by nearly 15,000 cases. Per million the picture shifts: 37.3 in 2025, under 2004's 41.1 and 2012's 39.6.
