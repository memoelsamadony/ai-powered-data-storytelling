# Global pertussis cases fell sharply in 2025, but remained the second highest since 2000

For most of this century, reported pertussis cases worldwide sat in a familiar band, roughly 117,000 to 250,000 cases a year between 2000 and 2019. Then came three unusually quiet years: 69,605 cases in 2020, 30,402 in 2021, the lowest in the series, and 64,313 in 2022.

2023 brought the count back to 163,400. Then 2024 did something else entirely: 941,893 reported cases, nearly four times the previous record set in 2012, and about 31 times the 2021 low. Incidence reached 137.1 per million, more than three times any other year in the record.

2025 looks calmer: 265,317 cases, a drop of about 72 percent from 2024. But calm is relative. That is still the second highest annual total in the 26 years covered here, above the old 2012 peak of 250,330. Measured against population, though, 2025's 37.3 per million sits below both 2004 and 2012.
