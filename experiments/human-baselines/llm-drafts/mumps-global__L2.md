# The global mumps rate has never been lower in 26 years of data

In 2000 the world reported 544,093 mumps cases, a rate of 316.8 per million people. By 2025 both numbers were far smaller: 234,954 cases, 41.2 per million. No year in this 26-year record comes in lower, and the rate has shrunk to just over an eighth of where it opened.

The route down was anything but tidy. Reported cases climbed to 726,638 in 2011, the biggest year on the list, and 2018 still logged 502,132. The following year they collapsed to 169,898, fewer than in any other year here.

That low didn't stick. Cases more than doubled off it, reaching 381,029 in 2022 and 387,586 in 2023, then eased to 245,062 and 234,954. The rate has been steadier: below 100 per million every year since 2019, a mark the series had hit just once before, in 2014.
