# The global mumps rate has fallen to its lowest in 26 years of data

In 2000, the world reported 544,093 mumps cases, a rate of 316.8 per million people. In 2025 the count was 234,954, and the rate was 41.2 per million. That is the lowest rate in the 26 years this record covers, about an eighth of where it started.

The path there was not smooth. Reported cases climbed to 726,638 in 2011, the highest year in the series, and were still above half a million in 2018. Then came 2019, when the count dropped to 169,898, the lowest in the record.

The years since have been choppier than that low suggests. Cases rose to 381,029 in 2022 and 387,586 in 2023, more than double the 2019 figure, before easing to 245,062 and then 234,954. The rate has stayed below 100 per million every year since 2019, a level the series had touched only once before, in 2014.
