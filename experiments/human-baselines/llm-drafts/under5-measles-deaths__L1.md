# Under-five measles deaths climbed 46 percent in 2021 to 151,463

Global measles deaths among children under five reached 151,463 in 2021. A year earlier the count was 103,901. The two years are 47,562 deaths apart, a jump of 46 percent. Nothing since 2019 has been higher.

Look back further and the direction reverses. Deaths fell from 756,332 in 2000 to 148,430 by 2012, roughly 80 percent off in twelve years. The decline stalled there. Every year since has landed between 103,901 and 190,190, averaging about 138,600 a year. At 151,463, 2021 sits just 3,033 above the 2012 total.

The peak of the series is 2002, at 848,272 deaths. Measured against that, 2021 comes in under a fifth. The floor is 2020's 103,901. Deaths have never dropped below 100,000 in any year the data covers.
