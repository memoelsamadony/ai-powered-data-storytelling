# Under-5 measles deaths rose 46 percent in 2021 to 151,463

Global measles deaths among children under five rose to 151,463 in 2021, an increase of 47,562 on the previous year. That is a rise of 46 percent from the 2020 total of 103,901, and the highest annual figure since 2019.

The rise comes after a decade of little movement. Between 2000 and 2012, annual deaths fell from 756,332 to 148,430, a drop of about 80 percent. Since 2012 the figure has fluctuated between 103,901 and 190,190, averaging roughly 140,000 a year. The 2021 total is slightly higher than the 2012 total.

The peak of the series is 2002, at 848,272 deaths. The 2021 figure is less than a fifth of that. The lowest year is 2020. In no year covered by the data have deaths fallen below 100,000.
