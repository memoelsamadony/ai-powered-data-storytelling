# A decade of falling child measles deaths, then nine years of nothing

In 2002 the global count of measles deaths among children under five reached 848,272, the highest in a series running from 2000 to 2021. Then it fell, year after year: by 2009 it was 279,411, a drop of two thirds. By 2012 it had reached 148,430.

That is where the descent ends. Nine years later, in 2021, the figure was 151,463, slightly higher, 3,033 more deaths than in 2012. In between, the line zigzags rather than falls: 190,190 in 2013, 104,776 in 2016, 168,887 in 2019.

The most recent two years capture the pattern in miniature. 2020 recorded 103,901 deaths, the lowest of any year in the series. 2021 recorded 151,463, up 46 percent, the highest count since 2019.

Measured against 2002, 2021 is still under a fifth of the peak. All of that decline, and a little more, was achieved by 2012.
