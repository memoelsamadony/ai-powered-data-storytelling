# The long fall in child measles deaths ended around 2012

The worst year in this series is 2002, with 848,272 measles deaths among children under five worldwide. What followed was a long slide. By 2009 the count stood at 279,411, roughly a third of the peak, and 2012 brought it down to 148,430.

The steady fall stops around then. Nine years later, in 2021, the count was 151,463, higher than 2012 by 3,033. In between the line zigzags rather than drops: 190,190 in 2013, 104,776 in 2016, 168,887 in 2019.

The last two years carry the whole story between them. 2020 came in at 103,901, lower than any other year in the run from 2000 to 2021. A year on, the count climbed 46 percent above that, higher than anything since 2019.

Set 2021 beside 2002 and it's under a fifth of the peak. Every bit of that fall, with a margin to spare, had already happened by 2012.
