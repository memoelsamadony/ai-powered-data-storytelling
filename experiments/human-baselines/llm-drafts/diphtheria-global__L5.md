# Diphtheria cases hit their highest reported level in 2025

The world reported 30,205 diphtheria cases in 2025, the highest total in a series that runs back to 2000. It was up 5,058 on 2024, a rise of about 20 percent.

The three biggest years on record are the three most recent. 2023 brought 24,782 cases, 2024 brought 25,147, and 2025 topped both. Together those three years account for 80,134 cases, more than the 50,010 reported across the whole decade from 2006 to 2015.

The count has not risen every year. It fell from 22,989 in 2019 to 10,137 in 2020, and stayed below 11,000 through 2022 before more than doubling.

The low point was 2006, with 4,333 cases and an incidence of 0.7 per million. The 2025 rate was 4 per million, close to the series high of 4.2 per million set in 2023. In 2000 the count was 11,625, well under half of last year's figure.
