# Diphtheria cases reached 30,205 in 2025, the most in 26 years of records

The world reported 30,205 diphtheria cases in 2025, the biggest total in a record that starts in 2000. The rise over 2024 came to 5,058 cases, about 20 percent.

Rank the 26 years by size and the top three are the most recent three. 2023 logged 24,782 cases and 2024 barely moved, to 25,147, before last year cleared both. Between them those three years hold 80,134 cases, against 50,010 for the whole decade from 2006 to 2015.

The line dips in places. Cases fell from 22,989 in 2019 to 10,137 in 2020, then stayed under 11,000 for two more years before more than doubling in 2023.

The quietest year on the list is 2006, with 4,333 cases and an incidence of 0.7 per million. Last year's rate was 4 per million, a shade below the series high of 4.2 set in 2023. In 2000 the count was 11,625, less than half of last year's total.
