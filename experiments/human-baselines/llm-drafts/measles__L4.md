# Measles cases are five and a half times their 2021 low, with coverage 11 points short

The world's measles count bottomed out in 2021 at 123,152, the lowest in a series that opens on 3,852,242 in 1980. Three years later it stood at 675,533, five and a half times the low.

2022 brought 206,775 cases. A year later the count stood at 669,083, so nearly all of the rise came in two steps. After that, almost nothing: 2024 sits 6,450 cases above 2023, a shade under 1%.

Vaccination tells a slower story. First-dose coverage ran at 16% in 1980, reached 73% by 1990, and crept to a peak of 86% in 2018 and 2019. It was 84% in 2024. Herd immunity needs about 95%, so 2024 falls 11 points short.

The 2024 country rows are why I lean on rates. India logged 18,530 cases to Nigeria's 14,999, the bigger count, yet Nigeria ran 65.8 per million against India's 12.9, about five times the rate. Nigeria's coverage was 57%, India's 97%, Germany's 96%.
