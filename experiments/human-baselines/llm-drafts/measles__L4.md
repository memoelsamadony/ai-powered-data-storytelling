# Measles cases are more than five times their 2021 low

In 2021 the world reported 123,152 measles cases, the lowest figure in a series that starts at 3,852,242 in 1980. Three years later the count stood at 675,533, roughly five and a half times the 2021 low.

The climb was uneven: 206,775 cases in 2022, then 669,083 in 2023. Between 2023 and 2024 the count barely moved, adding 6,450 cases, about 1%.

First-dose vaccination coverage has shifted far less. It climbed from 16% in 1980 to 73% by 1990, peaked at 86% in 2018 and 2019, and stood at 84% in 2024. Herd immunity needs about 95% first-dose coverage, so 2024 sits 11 points short.

The 2024 country figures show why rates matter more than counts. India reported 18,530 cases, more than Nigeria's 14,999, but India's rate was 12.9 per million against Nigeria's 65.8, roughly five times as high. Nigeria's coverage was 57%, India's 97%, Germany's 96%.
