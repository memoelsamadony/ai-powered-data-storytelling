# Global diphtheria cases hit 30,205 in 2025, a series high

Reported diphtheria cases worldwide hit 30,205 in 2025. No year in the series, which starts in 2000, recorded more. The rise on 2024 was 5,058 cases, about 20 percent.

Three years running, the count has topped 24,000. It stood at 10,027 in 2022. The next year it more than doubled, to 24,782, and 2024 added a little to that at 25,147. Measured against the 11,625 cases of 2000, the 2025 total is about 2.6 times as large.

The low came in 2006, at 4,333. It stayed under 8,000 through 2015. From 2015 it rose four years in a row, to 22,989 in 2019, before falling back to 10,137 in 2020 and 8,659 in 2021.

On rates, though, 2025 does not lead. Incidence ran at 4 per million, against 4.2 per million in 2023, which remains the highest rate in the series.
