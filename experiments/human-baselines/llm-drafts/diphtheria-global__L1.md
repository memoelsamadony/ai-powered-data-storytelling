# Global diphtheria cases rise to 30,205 in 2025, the highest since at least 2000

Reported diphtheria cases worldwide rose to 30,205 in 2025, the highest total in a series running back to 2000. The figure is up 5,058 on 2024, an increase of about 20 percent.

It is the third straight year above 24,000 cases. The count jumped from 10,027 in 2022 to 24,782 in 2023, then edged up to 25,147 in 2024. The 2025 total is about 2.6 times the 11,625 cases reported in 2000.

Cases bottomed at 4,333 in 2006 and stayed below 8,000 through 2015. They then rose in each of the next four years, reaching 22,989 in 2019, before falling to 10,137 in 2020 and 8,659 in 2021.

The rate was 4 per million in 2025, below the 4.2 per million recorded in 2023, the highest in the series.
