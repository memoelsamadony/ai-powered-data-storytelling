# Reported diphtheria cases hit their highest level in 26 years

Reported diphtheria cases worldwide reached 30,205 in 2025, the highest total in 26 years of data. It was the third consecutive year above 24,000, after 24,782 in 2023 and 25,147 in 2024. Those three years together account for 80,134 reported cases.

Rates tell a slightly different story. Incidence stood at 4 per million in 2025, up from 3.5 in 2024 but below the 4.2 per million recorded in 2023, the highest rate in the series. All four years at or above 3 per million fall within the last seven.

The contrast with the middle of the series is sharp. Between 2006 and 2013, incidence never exceeded 0.9 per million, sitting at 0.7 in five of those eight years. Reported cases in 2015 came to 4,535, less than a sixth of the 2025 figure.

These figures count reported cases, and give no indication of what lies behind the rise.
