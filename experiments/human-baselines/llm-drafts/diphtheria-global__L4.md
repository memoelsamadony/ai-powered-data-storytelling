# Reported diphtheria cases reach a 26-year high, but the rate does not

Reported diphtheria cases worldwide came to 30,205 in 2025, more than in any of the other 25 years on this record. It was the third year running above 24,000, after 24,782 in 2023 and 25,147 in 2024. Those three years hold 80,134 reported cases between them.

The rate doesn't quite follow. Incidence reached 4 per million in 2025, up from 3.5 a year earlier but short of the 4.2 per million logged in 2023, which stands as the series peak. Only four years reach 3 per million or more, and all of them fall in the last seven.

The middle of the record looks nothing like this. From 2006 to 2013, incidence never went past 0.9 per million and sat at 0.7 in five of those eight years. As recently as 2015 the count was 4,535, not even a sixth of last year's total.

These are reported cases, and nothing in the table explains the climb.
