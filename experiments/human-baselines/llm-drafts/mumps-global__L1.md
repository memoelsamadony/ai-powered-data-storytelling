# Global mumps rate falls to its lowest in a 26-year series

Reported mumps cases worldwide fell to 41.2 per million people in 2025, the lowest rate in a series that begins in 2000.

Reported cases totalled 234,954 that year, down 10,108, or 4.1 percent, from 245,062 in 2024. It was the second straight annual fall, after 387,586 cases in 2023. Only 2019, with 169,898 cases, was lower across the 26 years.

The longer trend is steeply down. Cases peaked at 726,638 in 2011. The 2025 total is roughly a third of that. Incidence stood at 316.8 per million in 2000, nearly eight times the 2025 rate.

The fall has not been smooth. Cases rose in both 2022 and 2023 from 241,742 in 2021, then dropped again in 2024 and 2025. The rate per million has fallen in each of the past two years.
