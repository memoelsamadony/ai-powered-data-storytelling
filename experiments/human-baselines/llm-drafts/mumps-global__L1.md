# Global mumps rate falls to a 26-year low of 41.2 per million

Mumps incidence worldwide fell to 41.2 per million in 2025. No year since the series began in 2000 has come in lower.

Reported cases totalled 234,954, down 10,108 from 245,062 in 2024, a fall of 4.1 percent. Two years of decline now, counting back from 387,586 in 2023. On case counts alone, only 2019 sits below 2025, at 169,898.

Across the full 26 years the drop is steep. Cases peaked at 726,638 in 2011. The 2025 total is about a third of that. The rate in 2000 was 316.8 per million, 7.7 times the 2025 figure.

It has not been a straight line down. Cases rose two years running from 241,742 in 2021, hitting 381,029 and then 387,586, before easing in 2024 and 2025. Incidence per million has fallen in each of the past two years.
